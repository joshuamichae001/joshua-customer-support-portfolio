# Support workflow

## 1. Receive and understand

Read the full message, identify the customer's goal, and acknowledge the impact in a calm, respectful tone.

## 2. Clarify safely

Ask concise questions for the minimum details needed. Use approved channels for account data; never request passwords, one-time codes, or full payment credentials.

## 3. Check the facts

Review the relevant order, account, or troubleshooting information in authorized systems. Record only useful facts and distinguish confirmed information from assumptions.

## 4. Resolve or escalate

Provide a policy-based solution when within scope. Escalate safety, security, billing, repeated failures, or out-of-policy issues with a concise summary of what has already been checked.

## 5. Explain next steps

Use plain language. Say what will happen, who owns the next action, and when the customer should expect an update. Avoid guarantees unless they are confirmed.

## 6. Close the loop

Confirm the outcome, check whether the customer needs anything else, and document the resolution in the approved system.

## Quality check before sending

- Did I answer the actual question?
- Is my tone respectful and specific?
- Are the facts verified?
- Did I protect private information?
- Are next steps and timing clear?
